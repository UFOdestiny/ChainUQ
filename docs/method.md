# Method

ChainUQ scores a chain-of-thought response `r = (step_1, ..., step_K, conclusion)` generated by a frozen
backbone LLM with a calibrated probability that its conclusion is correct. This page summarizes each part and
points to its implementation.

## Response format and spans

Every dataset prompt ends with a shared output contract (`Reasoning:` / `Step i:` / `Conclusion:`;
[`data/prompts.py`](../chainuq/data/prompts.py)). [`data/response.py`](../chainuq/data/response.py) parses the
response and aligns every step and the conclusion to a contiguous span of generated tokens; responses that do
not follow the contract are dropped (`python -m chainuq.pipeline.audit` reports the rates).

## Labels

- **Correctness `y`**: the main judge (gpt-oss-20b) compares the conclusion with the reference answer
  (`correct` task) on HotpotQA, MuSiQue, and bAbI; StrategyQA, GSM8K, and MATH-500 use answer matching
  ([`data/answers.py`](../chainuq/data/answers.py)), so no judge is involved.
- **Step labels** (`steps` task) and **chain verdict** (`final_blind` task): the judge sees the task input and
  the response but *never the reference answer* ([`pipeline/judge.py`](../chainuq/pipeline/judge.py)).

## Stage 1: UQ head

[`heads/chainuq.py`](../chainuq/heads/chainuq.py) reads cached features of one teacher-forced forward pass
([`pipeline/features.py`](../chainuq/pipeline/features.py)):

- **token branch** ([`heads/encoder.py`](../chainuq/heads/encoder.py)): per-token features (fused hidden states,
  top-k log-probabilities, local attention) are projected, the conclusion span is marked, a two-layer
  transformer contextualizes the response, and a span state (mean/max pooling, span-position statistics,
  claim-type embedding) is fused with a gated response summary;
- **layer branch**: all hidden layers at the token that predicts the conclusion and at the last conclusion
  token, mixed by learned softmax weights over layers;
- a sigmoid gate fuses both branches; **branch dropout** (p = 0.5) trains the output on each branch alone.

The head (width 112, under 2M parameters, like every probe baseline) is trained on HotpotQA with class-balanced
BCE on `y` ([`training.py`](../chainuq/training.py)).

**Privileged consistency supervision (PCS).** Two extra outputs are trained on the judge's answer-blind
labels, which are available during training only: a verdict output on the fused state (BCE to the chain
verdict) and step scores, obtained by encoding each step span as a reasoning-type claim (balanced BCE to the
step labels of two sampled steps per response). At inference the head needs no judge: it outputs the answer
logit `s`, a score for every step, and a predicted verdict.

## Stage 2: reasoning consistency-aware calibrator

[`evidence.py`](../chainuq/evidence.py) builds the evidence vector `u(r)` from the head scores of the conclusion
and the steps (`u_base`, `u_reason`), token uncertainty (`u_token`), chain structure and the
reasoning/conclusion feature gap (`u_gap`), and the judge's step labels (`u_judge`), plus token log-likelihood
and entropy of the conclusion span and the weakest step. It never receives the correctness label
([`tests/test_evidence.py`](../tests/test_evidence.py)).

[`calibration.py`](../chainuq/calibration.py) implements R-Log. An anchor score (the scalar calibrator with the
lowest cross-validated NLL) and a reasoning-conditioned score (L2 logistic regression on `[s; evidence]`) are
mixed with one weight per quantile bin and mapped by a final isotonic or Platt map. R-Log picks its capacity
(anchor-only isotonic, a strongly regularized logistic regression, cross-fitted R-Log at two regularization
strengths, or shallow gradient boosting) by inner 5-fold log-loss on the validation split it is fit on.

| Variant | Evidence | External model at inference |
|---|---|---|
| **ChainUQ** | `u(r)`, the judge's chain verdict, span features, the predicted verdict | judge (step labels + verdict) |
| **ChainUQ-A** | `u(r)` without `u_judge`, span features, the predicted verdict | none |

Every calibrator and decision threshold is fit on the validation split of the evaluated dataset and applied
once to its test split ([`evaluation.py`](../chainuq/evaluation.py)).

## Label-free target adaptation

Because the PCS targets never use the reference answer, the head can be adapted to a new domain from
unlabeled target questions: [`adaptation.py`](../chainuq/adaptation.py) fine-tunes the head for 300 steps on
source batches (answer + PCS loss) interleaved with target batches that contribute only the PCS loss on the
judge's labels of the target responses. Controls replace the target loss by the verdict as a pseudo-label,
keep only step labels, or drop the target loss entirely.
